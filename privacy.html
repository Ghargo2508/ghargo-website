Privacy Policy

Version 1 · last updated 22 October 2026

GharGo collects only the data it needs to help you find or list a PG, keeps it safe, and never sells it.

We remove hidden location data (EXIF/GPS) from photos you upload. We do not collect Aadhaar or PAN copies.

2. Who can see your data

  • Owners see only what is needed for a visit or a stay. Owners do not see the real mobile number of students who came through GharGo in the khata.
  • Students never see a PG's exact location, only how far it is from them.
  • GharGo's admin and team see data only to run the service.
  • Service providers we use (hosting, SMS for OTP, WhatsApp) get only what they need.
  • We share data with police or courts only when the law requires it.
  • We never sell your data.

3. How long we keep it

  • Login history: 180 days.
  • Account data: while your account is active. After you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep (such as payment records).

4. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to ghargo.app@gmail.com. You can turn off location for GharGo anytime in your phone settings; the app still works.

5. Safety

OTPs, login tokens and passwords are stored only in hashed form. Data travels over secure (HTTPS) connections.

6. Age

GharGo is only for people 18 and older.

7. Changes

We will show a notice in the app before big changes to this policy apply.
